### A gap in the earlier fit diagnostics, found while testing Run 5

torch's L-BFGS stops at `max_iter` iterations and also at `max_iter * 5 // 4` loss evaluations. `pilot_a.fit_head` flags only the first. The statements above that fits in Runs 2b, 3 and 4 "stopped by themselves before the limit" therefore mean only that they did not reach the iteration limit; some may have ended on the evaluation limit, and the stored diagnostics cannot tell. `val_compare.py` uses its own `fit_linear`, which has the same objective, optimiser and settings (it returns bit-identical weights on the CPU) and records evaluations and both caps. Run 5 refits the pilot's ordinary-head fits at l2 0.01, 0.001 and 0.0001, so it shows whether the evaluation limit was reached for those. It cannot show how the pilot's prior-in-loss or reweighted fits ended.
